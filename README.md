## 🔬 Research

Pixelchemy Research explores efficient and controllable generative AI, with a focus on:

- 🎨 **Generative Vision** — image generation, video synthesis, style transfer, super-resolution
- ⚡ **Training-Free Generation** — controllable generation and adaptation without retraining
- 🚀 **Efficient Sampling** — few-step diffusion, flow matching, and efficient inference
- 🎧 **Multimodal AI** — audio-driven generation, talking-face synthesis, and diffusion language models
